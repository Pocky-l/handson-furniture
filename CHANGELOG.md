# Changelog

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-17
- Coolers, storage cabinets and chests of drawers open by hand too, in every wood and colour, and hold more than their
  menus.
- A cooler's lid swings up and rocks back onto its hinge, and what you put in lies on its floor where you set it.
- Microwaves open by hand: things stand on the plate, which turns while the microwave heats.
- Stoves open by hand: things stand on the oven's floor and rack, and three bake at once wherever they stand.
- Microwaves and stoves show what they hold through their windows, also in the world.
- The freezer, the microwave and the stove have their own power switch on them, in their finish; the microwave's green
  lamp shows it is on.
- The freezer freezes what you put in wherever it stands, and what comes out frozen stands beside it.
- Refurbished's wrench links furniture to power again: with it in hand, using the furniture no longer opens it.
- What a hopper, a command or another mod puts into furniture shows inside at once, for everyone near.
- Things that come in by a shift-click or a hopper stand where you can see them.
- Nothing goes through the shut glass door of a microwave or an oven.
- From the fridge, S goes down to its freezer, and W goes back up.

## [1.0.0]

The first release: the kitchen furniture of [MrCrayfish's Furniture Mod: Refurbished](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/refurbished-furniture) opens right where it stands, with no menu screen.

- The fridge, the freezer under it and the kitchen storage cabinets open their doors, and what they hold stands on their shelves and in the fridge door's bins.
- Kitchen drawers have an upper and a lower drawer, each pulled out on its own with its contents lying inside.
- Doors come unstuck from their seal, swing part way open on hinges against the body and knock softly against the frame when shut.
- The camera faces the furniture you use and follows a drawer as it comes out; a cabinet is seen from the side, past its open door.
- Put things anywhere on a shelf, turned and sized as you like, even on top of each other, and they stay there for everyone to see.
- Things have weight and stop where they land: a stone block shoves an apple aside, a block set on its edge falls over.
- No slot limit, only the room on the shelves: whatever is crowded over the edge falls out into your inventory or onto the floor.
- What you carry hangs from your fingers and never knocks over what stands there: it rises onto things or keeps beside them.
- Things in the fridge and the freezer are smaller, so two fit one above the other under a shelf.
- Cans, bottles and other 3D food from mods stand upright, and a lantern or torch on a shelf lights up the inside.
- Shift-click a stack to put it away or take it back, and point at a stack to see how many it holds.
- Hoppers, breaking the furniture and its menu for players without the mod keep working as before.
- Install it on the server and the client, Forge or Fabric.
